## What Was Measured

Lifetime earnings differences between program beneficiaries and counterfactual non-participants, using India-specific data (PLFS 2023-24, ILO 2024, published Mincer regressions). Career horizon: 40 years, discounted at 5%.

## What the BCR INCLUDES

- Lifetime earnings differentials (formal vs. informal sector)
- Sector-specific experience premiums and wage growth
- Education quality premium (RTE Mincer chain)
- Apprenticeship opportunity costs
- 5% social discounting

## What the BCR Does NOT Include

- Tax revenue, health benefits, intergenerational effects, welfare savings
- These are real but not yet quantified with RWF-specific data

## Wider Social Value (NOT Included in BCR -- Directional Only)

The following outcomes are **real and economically significant** but are NOT included in the headline BCR. They are presented separately to show the conservative nature of our estimates.

### Tax Revenue

Formal sector workers contribute to the exchequer through:
- **Income tax**: Formal wages above Rs 3 lakhs are taxable (~5-10% effective rate for entry-level)
- **EPF contributions**: 12% employer + 12% employee = 24% of basic salary
- **ESI contributions**: 4.75% of wages for workers earning below Rs 21,000/month
- **Estimated additional value**: 10-15% on top of wage BCR

### Health Benefits via ESI

Formal employment provides Employees' State Insurance (ESI) coverage:
- Free healthcare for worker + dependents (spouse, 2 children)
- Maternity benefit (26 weeks paid leave)
- Disability and death benefits
- **Estimated value**: Rs 8,000-15,000/year per formal worker (ESI cost per beneficiary)

### Intergenerational Effects

Children of formally employed parents benefit from:
- Higher household income → better nutrition, health, education spending
- Parental education aspirations → higher educational attainment in next generation
- Urban residence → access to better schools and healthcare
- **Evidence**: Each additional year of parental education is associated with 0.3-0.5 additional years of child education (global meta-analyses)

### Reduced Welfare Dependency

Formal employment reduces dependence on:
- MGNREGA (rural employment guarantee)
- PDS (subsidized food distribution)
- State welfare schemes for BPL families
- **Estimated fiscal savings**: Rs 5,000-15,000/year per household that transitions from informal to formal

> **Important**: These wider social value estimates are **directional only**. They have NOT been empirically measured for RWF beneficiaries and should NOT be added to the BCR. A tracer study and SROI methodology would be needed to credibly quantify these effects.

## Confidence Level & Scenario Range
